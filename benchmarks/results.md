Core: `0.1.0-phase2`, 48 kHz mono, RNNoise pinned at `70f1d25`.
Test signals: synthetic pseudo-speech + noise (fan/keyboard/babble/traffic/hum),
generated by `make_test_signals.py` (deterministic seeds). Run on this machine;
see `run_eval.py` / `measure_latency.py` to reproduce.

## Notes (honest interpretation)

- Stationary low-frequency noise (fan, traffic, AC-like) is suppressed strongly (~19–23 dB SNR gain).
- Babble (speech-like) is deliberately preserved — that is the intended voice-protection behavior.
- Mains hum (50 Hz) mostly passes RNNoise's high-pass by design; keyboard transients are kept.
- Clipping counts on 0 dB mixes come from the synthetic mix itself saturating int16, not from the engine.
- VB-CABLE end-to-end loopback latency: UNVERIFIED (driver not installed in this environment).
